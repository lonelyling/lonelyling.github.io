About Me

I am a Software Engineering undergraduate at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), advised by Prof. Tao Huang, and expect to graduate in 2027.

My research experience includes an internship with Prof. Xiaofeng Gao at SJTU on cloud workload prediction, and an internship with Zhuoran Lu at The University of Hong Kong (HKU) on AI-assisted storytelling. In summer 2026, I was a visiting student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Xiaojuan Ma.

My interests lie in efficient world models, human–computer interaction, and data mining. I am interested in making AI both more computationally efficient and more useful to people—from efficient video generation to interactive systems that support creativity and communication.

Ultimately, I hope to develop AI technologies that serve people and make their benefits more widely accessible.

Research Interests

  • Efficient World Models: linear attention and efficient video generation.
  • Human–Computer Interaction: human–AI collaboration, interactive storytelling, and communication.
  • Data Mining: time-series forecasting and spatio-temporal modeling for cloud workloads.
