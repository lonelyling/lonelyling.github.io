About Me

I am a Software Engineering undergraduate at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), where I am advised by Prof. Tao Huang and expect to graduate in 2027. In summer 2026, I was a visiting student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Xiaojuan Ma.

My research interests span efficient world models, human–computer interaction, and data mining. I hope to advance AI technologies that serve people and make the benefits of the AI era more widely accessible.
